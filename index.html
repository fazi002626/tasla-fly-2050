Tasla Fly 2050 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Tasla Fly 2050 ("the game") is an offline game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect, or send any personal information. It has no account sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not use the internet.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, stages, stars, cars, items you bought with in-game coins, settings such as sound and vibration, and the text of worlds you create in the AI World Creator) is saved only on your own phone. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

"AI" features

NOVA, the AI World Creator and the AI rivals run entirely on your phone. Text you type into the AI World Creator is processed on the device to build a level and is not sent anywhere.

Purchases

All items in the shop are bought with coins earned inside the game. There are no real-money purchases.

Permissions

The game may use vibration for haptic feedback. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
